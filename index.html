Vis 2 — Fuel Price Cycle Patterns

Australian cities show very different fuel pricing cycles. WA is highly predictable with short cycles, while eastern states experience longer and more volatile pricing patterns.

Vis 3 — Transport Affordability Distribution (Melbourne vs Geelong)

This faceted density plot compares how transport affordability is distributed over time between Melbourne and Geelong. Melbourne consistently shows higher transport cost pressure as a percentage of income, while Geelong remains lower but gradually converges upward. Fuel share contributes a stable but significant portion of total transport costs.

Vis 4 — Most Expensive States (Ranked)

The graph below displays a comparison of the fuel prices for various states in Australia by utilizing the combination of the layered histogram and the cumulative histogram. This particular representation helps the audience to recognize that there are two factors to be considered: the number of states in a certain price category and the accumulation of states with increasing prices. It is worth emphasizing that fuel prices are not equally distributed in all regions; therefore, fuel prices group into certain bands due to their geographical location and policies implemented in them.
